Welcome to the jungle

Rules are boring.
We prefer chaos.

A late-night spot in Southampton’s cultural quarter for cocktail lovers and music hunters. Early doors it’s relaxed, with cocktails and no crush. Later it turns into a proper night out, with the DJ booth raised above the entrance looking out over the dancefloor.

01

Cocktails

Over 40 cocktails, from house classics to The OG’s, a set named after the family. 2‑for‑1 all day every Friday.

02

Grumpy Grill

Best known for Jungle Burgers and Dirty Fries. Beef, chicken and plant-based burgers, loaded nachos and fries.

03

Live sport

Shown live across 11 screens. Call ahead if you want to check a particular fixture is on.

Good to know

Questions.

Something not answered here? Call 023 8033 7171 or email info@grumpy-monkey.co.uk.

What time are you open?

Fridays 4pm till 1am and Saturdays 12pm till 1am. Sunday to Thursday the venue is available for private hire.

Do I need to book a table?

You can book a table online any time. It’s worth doing for weekends and bigger groups.

Do you serve food?

Yes. We’re a bar, lounge and kitchen, best known for our Jungle Burgers and Dirty Fries.

Do you do bottomless brunch?

Yes. Brunch Club runs every Saturday and we’re taking bookings. Tickets are sold through FIXR.

Do you show live sport?

Yes, live across 11 screens. Give us a ring if you want to check we’re showing a particular fixture.

Is there outdoor seating?

There are a few picnic benches out front. They’re the first thing to fill up when the sun’s out.

Can I hire the venue?

Hire the main room, the mez (while we’re open), the side room or the whole venue. Buffet menus and drinks packages are available on request.

Do you sell gift vouchers?

Yes. Buy them online. They’re valid for 12 months from the date of purchase.
