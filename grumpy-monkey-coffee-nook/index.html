The nook

Small shop.
Big mornings.

A busy local favourite with a friendly crew and a queue that often runs out the door. Regulars drive past other cafés to get their coffee here.

Up before you

Open at 5am on weekdays and 6am on weekends, every day of the year except Christmas Day and New Year’s Day.

A brownie, on us

Every coffee comes with a small piece of homebaked brownie. It’s the thing guests mention most.

Dogs welcome

Pet friendly, with puppaccinos and dog biscuits for your four-legged regular.
